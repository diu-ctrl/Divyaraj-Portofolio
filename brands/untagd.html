The AI Creative Workflow

A disciplined six-stage production loop turning initial briefs into polished cinematic output with human creative intent guiding every generation.

Stage 01
Research & Moodboarding

Deconstructing client briefs, analyzing reference cinema, lighting styles, environment physics, and building curated moodboards for stylistic alignment.

Stage 02
Concept & Styleframes

Generating foundational visual keyframes, establishing color grading palettes, character/vehicle consistency, and architectural horizon aesthetics.

Stage 03
Prompt Architecture

Engineering precise, multi-layered prompt formulas combining lens descriptions, lighting angles, motion vectors, and negative weights.

Stage 04
AI Video Synthesis

Iterative Image-to-Video and Text-to-Video generation across multiple AI engines, steering camera velocity, depth of field, and dynamic subject interaction.

Stage 05
Post-Production & Rhythm

Importing raw generative takes into Premiere Pro, cutting to tempo, speed ramping, adding grain texture, and layering punchy sound effects.

Stage 06
Final Delivery

Exporting multi-format video assets calibrated for high-impact social media distribution, digital billboards, and brand campaigns.

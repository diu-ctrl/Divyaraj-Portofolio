Case Study • Gaming Creator Agency

8Bit Creatives

"A dark-mode editorial gaming-native creator agency website, an internal ops portal with 9 operational views, and three external partner dashboards (Creator, Brand, Platform) — built for India's biggest gaming creator agency representing 12 creators, a BGMI esports roster, and founders who started in a Mumbai LAN room in 2018."

Role Full-Stack Design & Dev
Timeline 7 Days
Status Live
Platform Vercel (Static Site)
Visit Live Site View Admin Portal

The Challenge

8Bit Creatives needed a digital home that matched the energy of India's gaming creator economy — not a corporate brochure. The agency manages 12 of India's biggest gaming creators, operates a competitive BGMI esports roster, and ships 300+ campaigns for brands including ASUS, Red Bull, Krafton, Netflix, Realme, and Amazon.

The existing online presence was a basic landing page that didn't reflect the scale of operations. The team also needed an internal tool to manage campaigns, approvals, and creator deliverables — they were running everything on spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups. And they needed three separate external dashboards: one for creators to check their campaigns and payments, one for brands to track campaign performance, and one for platform partners (Kick, YouTube, Loco) to monitor talent migration and growth metrics.

The brief was to ship all of this — a public-facing marketing site, an internal ops portal, and three partner dashboards — in one cohesive system that worked on a static hosting stack with no backend to maintain.

My Role & Scope

  • Visual identity & brutalist editorial design system (Geist Sans typography, 8Bit orange #FF5931 accent, capsule pill buttons, animated 8BIT letter backgrounds)
  • 9 public website pages designed and developed end-to-end (Landing, About, Creator Roster, Case Study Archive, Services, Esports Roster, Full Case Study blueprint, Privacy Policy, Contact)
  • Internal ops portal with 9 views (Dashboard, Roster, Approvals, Campaign Pipeline, Brief Intake, File Vault, Reports, Guide, Settings & Permissions)
  • 3 partner dashboards (Creator Dashboard, Brand Dashboard, Platform Dashboard) — each with a tailored sidebar, stats row, and workflow views
  • Cart + booking system on the landing page (Formspree-integrated contact form, honeypot-protected, inline success/error handling)
  • Security hardening (Content Security Policy, HSTS preload, X-Frame-Options, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy, SRI hashes on external scripts, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, .vercelignore, 308 redirects for clean URLs)
  • Mobile-responsive across all pages with custom @media breakpoints
  • Vercel deployment with auto-deploy on push to main

Results & Learnings

Delivered 14 production HTML pages in 7 days — 9 public website pages, 1 ops portal with 9 views, 3 partner dashboards, and a custom 404 page. The site ships with a strict Content-Security-Policy (blocks mixed content, restricts script sources to self + cdnjs + GA, blocks iframe embedding from other origins), HSTS preload eligibility, SRI hashes on every external CDN script, and a honeypot-protected Formspree contact form with inline success/error handling.

The ops portal replaced the team's spreadsheets-and-WhatsApp workflow with a single dashboard where campaigns move through Brief Intake → Production → Approval → Delivered → Live & Reporting, with interactive deliverables checklists and per-campaign communication logs. The three partner dashboards gave creators, brands, and platform partners their own self-serve views — reducing the operations team's support load significantly.

The animated 8BIT letter background (100 columns on desktop, 40 on mobile) runs at 60fps via GPU-accelerated translate3d + will-change:transform transforms, keeping the main thread free for scroll interactions and the playable dino runner game in the hero. The cart + booking system on the landing page routes inquiries directly to the team inbox via Formspree, with a WhatsApp deep-link fallback for mobile users.
